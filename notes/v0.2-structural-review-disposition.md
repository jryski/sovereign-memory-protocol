# v0.2 Structural Review Disposition

_Status: review evidence; not protocol authority_

This note records how the additive v0.2 candidate addresses the structural blockers identified against the preserved v0.1 opening package. It does not accept the candidate or authorize merge.

## Core versus profiles

`spec/08-immutability-and-chain-of-custody-v0.2.md` defines logical fields, bindings, states, claim limits, and security floors without selecting a wire encoding, digest, signature suite, Merkle construction, witness provider, storage engine, or deployment. Concrete canonical bytes, algorithms, tree/proof rules, witnesses, packaging, and resource ceilings remain versioned profile concerns.

The preserved v0.1 document contains concrete proposal material and remains `NEEDS REVISION`; it is historical review evidence, not the v0.2 core.

## Result registry

`spec/07-errors.md` is the single authoritative v0.2 error-class registry. The
additive v0.2 case catalog uses registered uppercase classes. `spec/05` separately
registers dimension statuses and operation dispositions. Ordinary pending,
partial, stale, quarantined, conflict, incomplete, or unknown states are not
error classes unless a named claim policy is violated and the underlying state
remains explicit. Lowercase outcomes in the preserved v0.1 matrix are frozen
historical diagnostic spellings; they are not registered v0.2 classes and must
not appear in promoted v0.2 fixture expectations.

## Verification versus operation disposition

`spec/05-verification.md` and `CST-VER-009` require separate
`dimension_status`, `operation_disposition`, and zero or more `error_class`
values. Operation acceptance, rejection, conflict, pending, no-op, partial
acceptance, and quarantine are not verification evidence.

## Privacy transformation

`CST-ERA-009` prohibits rewriting, recanonicalizing, or replacing original
canonical event bytes and commitments. A privacy-safe derivative must remain a
distinct artifact bound to the original commitment, transformation profile,
removed-field classifications, authority, and assurance loss. It cannot be
represented as the original event.

## Verification dimensions

`spec/05-verification.md` publishes the authoritative core dimension registry. The v0.2 traceability ledger uses requirement-family grouping labels and maps every family to one authoritative dimension. Families are not competing dimension identifiers.

## Repeatable repository checks

`.github/workflows/review-package.yml` runs the dependency-free public validator seam at `scripts/validate_repository.py --root . --json` and its unit tests. The validator checks manifest closure and hashes, relative links, sanitation and secret patterns, generated line prefixes, status conflicts, requirement/traceability closure, registered result codes, fixture identifiers, requirement-family mappings, and authoritative dimensions. CI records the exact commit, tool versions, validation receipt, and receipt checksum.

These checks validate repository review-package integrity only. They do not materialize canonical vectors, execute an SMP implementation, or establish protocol conformance.
